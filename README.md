## Engineering approach

- Translate real workflows into product behaviour and clear system boundaries.
- Use AI where it solves a specific problem, with explicit integration and operational constraints.
- Combine hands-on engineering with architecture, delivery and technical leadership.
- Use automated checks and direct product verification to assess what actually works.
- Distinguish implemented capabilities, prototypes and planned work.
